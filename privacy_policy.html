Privacy Policy

App: Megachurch Tycoon: Idle Pastor
Package: com.sycamorehillstudios.megachurch
Publisher: Sycamore Hill Studios
Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Megachurch Tycoon (“the App,” “we,” “our”) handles information when you use our Android game. Your game progress and settings live on your device. The App also uses three Google services that collect some data: Google AdMob (optional rewarded video ads), Firebase Analytics (gameplay statistics) and Google Play Billing (purchases). Each is described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for, access, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your real name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your photos, contacts, microphone, or camera
  • Your precise (GPS) location
  • Your call log or SMS history
  • Any health, financial, or biometric data

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device using Android’s DataStore mechanism. We do not receive this data. It is only copied off the device if you use Android’s own backup feature:

  • Your in-game progress (tithes, followers, ministries, venues, Sainthood tier, Eons, Covenants, achievements, etc.)
  • Your in-app settings (sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, notifications)
  • Which in-app products you own (for example Private Sanctuary Access or an active Prosperity Pass)
  • Your daily login streak, session count, and reward cooldowns

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in your device’s settings, deletes this local data.

3. Gameplay Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The App uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to understand how the game is played so we can balance difficulty and fix bugs. Firebase Analytics collects:

  • An app instance ID: a random identifier created for this installation of the App;
  • Gameplay events, for example “session started,” “venue unlocked,” “revival performed,” “reward claimed,” and “purchase completed” (product name only);
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, country or region (derived from your IP address), app version, and session length.

Analytics uses Google’s consent mode. Analytics storage and advertising signals start out denied. Where consent is not required by law, they are turned on once the App confirms that. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, they follow the choices you make in Google’s consent form (see section 4), and you can change them at any time.

Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google LLC and sent over encrypted HTTPS. Event-level data is kept for no more than 14 months; aggregated reports may be kept longer. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded video ads only. You choose to watch one by tapping a reward tile, and you get an in-game reward when it finishes. The App has no banner, interstitial, or app-open ads. Ads are limited to content rated PG or milder. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and the AdMob SDK collects:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (resettable, and deletable on newer Android versions);
  • Your IP address, which is used to estimate your approximate location (such as your country or city);
  • Device and app diagnostics: device model, operating system, app version, and performance and crash data from the ad SDK;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, tapped, or watched to the end.

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, to personalize ads where you allow it, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the App shows Google’s consent form, where you can accept, refuse, or choose individual purposes. You can change your choice at any time in the App under More → Privacy & data → Privacy options.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device’s Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You never have to watch an ad. Players who buy Private Sanctuary Access or subscribe to the Prosperity Pass claim ad rewards instantly without watching a video.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers optional in-app purchases: Miracle packs, the one-time Private Sanctuary Access unlock, and the monthly Prosperity Pass subscription, which renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your card number, billing address, or any payment credential. Google returns the product and a purchase token to the App so it can deliver what you bought.

6. Google Play Services

Because the App is distributed through Google Play, Google Play services may collect limited diagnostic information (install statistics, crash and ANR reports) independently of the App. This collection is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Who Receives Your Data

The data described in sections 3 to 6 is collected and processed by Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase, and Google Play). We do not share it with anyone else, and we do not sell your personal information for money. Under some US state laws, the use of your advertising ID for personalized ads may count as “sharing”; you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization as described in section 4.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is rated Teen and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Similar Laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • Withdraw or change ad and analytics consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): More → Privacy & data → Privacy options.
  • Erase local data: uninstall the App, or clear its storage in your device’s settings.
  • Limit advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Make any other request, for example deletion of Firebase Analytics data linked to your installation: email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Data Security

All network requests the App makes are sent over HTTPS/TLS. Local save data is stored in Android’s private application sandbox and is not readable by other apps on your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable law. When we do, we will update the “Effective date” at the top of this page.
